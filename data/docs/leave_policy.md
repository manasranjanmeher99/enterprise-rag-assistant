# Acme Corp Leave and Remote Work Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the employee's manager. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive days. Employees should notify their manager before the start of their working day.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time. Working remotely from another country for more than 14 days requires approval from HR.
